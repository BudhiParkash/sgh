## 2. Rules for every creative

**Do**
- Say "Independent fan platform — not affiliated with KRAFTON" in descriptions.
- Mention **18+ only** when promoting Custom Rooms or rewards.
- Say rewards are **official voucher codes**, given while stock lasts, as per the SGC Rewards Terms.
- Use your own designs, AI images you generated, or your own recorded gameplay.
- Link the official source when talking about a tournament.

**Don't**
- Don't put BGMI / PUBG / KRAFTON logos or official artwork on website images or thumbnails that look official.
- Don't say "free UC hack", "unlimited UC" or "guaranteed Royal Pass". Say "win SGC coins, redeem for UC vouchers".
- No betting, entry fees, wallets, paid slots or "pay to join".
- No mods, hacks, cheats or "secret glitch" content.
- Don't invent tournament dates, prize pools or sensitivity "test results".

## 3. Features and content ideas

### 3.1 Shivanshi Custom Rooms (main feature, newest)
Pages: /custom-rooms, /custom-rooms/[room]

What it is: free custom matches run by Shivanshi.
- **Joining:** players sign in with Google, build a squad, and the captain registers the team.
- **Room details:** each room has a mode (solo, duo or squad), TPP or FPP, maps such as Erangel and Livik, limited slots with a progress bar, and a LIVE badge while playing.
- **Room ID and password:** these appear on the room page shortly before the match.
- **Results:** standings and winners are published after the match. Scoring is placement points 10, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 1, plus 1 point per kill.
- **Prizes:** winners get SGC coins.
- **Home page:** rooms also appear on the home page, above tournaments.

Video ideas
- "How to join Shivanshi's free custom room in 60 seconds": screen recording of sign in → squad → register.
- Match-day live stream with the room page on screen, showing slots filling up.
- Post-match Short: top 3 squads, kills, points table reveal.
- "Squad of the week" winner highlights.

Image ideas
- Match poster: map, mode, date, time IST, slots, "FREE · 18+".
- "Slots almost full" story with the slot bar at 22/25.
- Winners card: rank, squad name, points, SGC won.
- Room featured image for the website, with no game logos.

Hook lines
- "Free custom room tonight — only 25 slots"
- "No entry fee. Just skill."
- "Your squad vs mine, Erangel, 8 PM"
- "Room ID drops on the site 15 minutes before"

Note: never post Room IDs publicly; they're shown only on the site.

### 3.2 Daily spin, streaks and SGC rewards
Pages: /rewards, /account

What it is:
- **Daily spin:** signed-in players spin a wheel once a day to earn SGC coins. It resets at midnight IST.
- **Streaks:** playing on consecutive days builds a streak with bonus coins.
- **Other coins:** coins are also won in Custom Rooms.
- **Rewards store:** coins are redeemed for official voucher codes such as UC and Royal Pass. The team sends each voucher after checking the request.

Video ideas
- "I spun the Shivanshi wheel for 7 days — here's what I got" (honest results).
- How SGC coins work: spin → streak → rooms → redeem, in 45 seconds.
- Real redemption proof (with permission, blur the code).

Image ideas
- Spin wheel graphic in violet and cyan: "Spin daily · free".
- Streak calendar graphic: Day 1 → Day 7 with bonus marks.
- Rewards store poster with this month's rewards.

Hook lines
- "One free spin every day"
- "Don't break your streak"
- "Play, collect SGC, redeem for UC vouchers"

Note: take the real coin amounts from Admin → Settings → Play. Never make up odds or amounts.

### 3.3 Tournament Hub
Pages: /tournaments, /tournaments/calendar, /tournaments/upcoming, /ongoing, /completed, /rumours

What it is: every BGMI tournament in one place, in a calendar view.
- **Lists:** tournaments are sorted into upcoming, ongoing, completed, cancelled and rumours.

Video ideas

- "Rumour or real?" series: check a viral claim against the official source.
- How to register for an official tournament, step by step.

Image ideas
- Calendar carousel: one slide per event with date and prize pool.
- "Verified" vs "Rumour" stamp graphics.
- Trophy graphic as the recurring tournament visual.

Hook lines
- "Every BGMI tournament, one calendar"

### 3.4 Sensitivity hub (biggest search traffic)
Pages: /sensitivity, /sensitivity/devices/[brand]/[phone], /sensitivity/codes, /sensitivity/compare

What it is: "Find Your Sensitivity". Players pick their phone brand and model and get a setup for it.
- **Labels:** each setup is labelled tested, community or baseline, so players know how reliable it is.
- **Extra tools:** gun-wise setups, ready-to-paste sensitivity codes, and a compare tool.

Video ideas
- "Best sensitivity for [phone model]": one Short per popular phone, linking its page.
- Live test: Shivanshi tries a setup in TDM and rates recoil control. This can become a "tested" setup.
- How to copy a sensitivity code into BGMI in 30 seconds.
- Compare two phones' setups using the compare tool.

Image ideas
- Phone-model card: phone outline + "Sensitivity code inside".
- Settings table graphic (camera, ADS, gyro) in the mono font.
- "Tested / Community / Baseline" label explainer post.

Hook lines
- "Your phone, your sensitivity"
- "Zero recoil? Try this code"
- "Tested on a real device, not guessed"

Note: only call a setup "tested" when the owner really tested it (tester, date, device, game version).

## 4. Sizes and formats

| Where | Size (px) | Ratio | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube video | 1920 × 1080 | 16:9 | Guides, match streams, weekly roundups |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280 × 720 | 16:9 | Max 4 big words, face or object |
| Shorts / Reels / Stories | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Room announcements, sensitivity codes, results |
| Instagram post / carousel | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | Guide summaries, winners cards, calendar |
| Website featured image | 1200 × 630 | ~1.9:1 | Articles, tournaments, rooms (Admin → Media) |
| Facebook post | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 | Room and tournament posters |

- Keep text inside the middle 80% of the frame so app buttons don't cover it.
- Every website image needs real alt text. The site makes smaller versions automatically.
