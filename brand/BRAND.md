# Shivanshi Gaming Hub — Brand Guide (v1, 2026-11-23)

All assets in this folder are original works created for Shivanshi Gaming Hub. They do not use or imitate KRAFTON, Tencent, BGMI or PUBG logos, typefaces or game art.

## Logo
| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo-mark.svg` | app icon, favicon, avatar, social profile picture |
| `logo-mark-mono.svg` | single colour (`currentColor`), for watermarks on sensitivity cards and print |
| `logo-horizontal.svg` | headers, documents. On the website the wordmark is live text (better for SEO and accessibility); this SVG is for external use and depends on Space Grotesk being installed. Outline the text before sending it to printers |

**Concept:** an angular "S" (Shivanshi) traced like a movement path inside a hexagonal frame (a tactical, esports feel). The cyan stroke at the end is the "aim trail", and it's the only place cyan appears in the logo.

**Clear space:** at least 25% of the mark's width on every side. **Minimum size:** 20px (digital), 8mm (print).

**Don't:** recolour the gradient, add glows or drop shadows, rotate it, place it on busy imagery without the dark hex fill, or put it next to game publisher logos in a way that implies partnership.

## Typography
- **Space Grotesk** (700/600) — display and headings
- **Inter** (400/500/600) — body and UI
- **JetBrains Mono** (500) — sensitivity values, share codes
All three are free under the SIL Open Font License.

## Voice
Confident, helpful and honest. We say "tested on" and "as announced by". We never say "guaranteed", "best ever" or "official".
